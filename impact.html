The Impact of Poor Waste Disposal

Waste left in open spaces can affect people, wildlife and the natural environment. Keeping Oodi clean is a shared responsibility.

♡

Health Risks

Unmanaged rubbish can attract flies, rats and other pests. Bad smells and unsafe waste can make neighbourhoods unpleasant and unhealthy.

💧

Land and Water Pollution

Rain can carry litter into drains and waterways. Some waste can contaminate soil and harm plants and animals.

☀

Blocked Drainage

Plastic bags and other rubbish can block drainage channels, slowing water flow and increasing local flooding risks.

🍃

Harm to Wildlife

Animals may swallow plastic or become trapped in discarded items, which can injure them.

⌂

Unpleasant Surroundings

Illegal dumping makes streets and open areas look neglected and can reduce community pride.

♻

Wasted Resources

Materials that could be reused or recycled are lost when all rubbish is mixed together.

Protect Oodi for everyone

Small changes—using bins, separating waste, reducing single-use items and joining clean-ups—can help prevent these impacts.
