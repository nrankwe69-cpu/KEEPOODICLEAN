Dispose of Waste Properly

A cleaner environment is a healthier, safer and happier community.

Learn How  →

What Can You Do?

Small actions make a big difference. Here are simple ways to dispose of waste properly:

▤

Use Bins

Put waste in the right bins. Do not throw it on the ground, roadsides or open spaces.

♻

Separate Waste

Sort your waste into recyclables, organic and non-recyclable materials.

🍃

Compost Organic Waste

Food scraps and garden waste can be composted to make healthy soil.

♧

Recycle

Paper, plastic, glass and metal can be recycled and reused where facilities are available.

♟

Keep It Clean

Make sure your surroundings, streets and drainage areas are free from waste.

Why It Matters

♡

Better Health

Proper waste disposal helps reduce pests, disease risks and bad smells.

🍃

Cleaner Environment

It reduces pollution and helps keep our land, water and air clean.

♧

Stronger Communities

A clean Oodi creates a safer and more beautiful place for everyone.

🌱

A Sustainable Future

Responsible waste habits help protect resources for future generations.
