Simple Solutions for a Cleaner Oodi

Build good habits at home, at school and in public spaces. A cleaner community begins with each of us.

1. Reduce Waste

Choose reusable bags and bottles, avoid unnecessary packaging and buy only what you need.

2. Reuse What You Can

Repair, refill or donate usable items instead of throwing them away immediately.

3. Separate Waste at Home

Keep recyclable materials, food scraps and other rubbish separate where practical. Follow local collection guidance.

4. Use Approved Collection Points

Place household waste in designated bins or collection areas. Never dump rubbish in drains, roadsides or open land.

5. Compost Organic Waste

Where suitable, compost fruit and vegetable scraps and garden waste to create useful soil material.

6. Join Community Clean-ups

Work with neighbours, schools and local groups to remove litter safely and encourage good habits.

Your daily clean-up checklist

  • Put rubbish in a bin with a lid when possible.
  • Keep waste away from drains and water sources.
  • Do not burn mixed household rubbish.
  • Keep recyclables clean and dry when appropriate.
  • Tell the relevant local service about overflowing bins or dumping.
  • Encourage family members to follow the same habits.

Remember the 3 Rs

Reduce the waste you create.

Reuse items before discarding them.

Recycle suitable materials through available local services.
